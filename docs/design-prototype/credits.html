Cover sources and rights

Three covers were copied without modification from the locally downloaded Standard Ebooks editions below. The prototype displays the complete artwork with object-fit: contain, without cropping or recoloring. No text from these books is included.

Each edition's embedded rights statement identifies source artwork as believed public domain in the United States, and dedicates Standard Ebooks contributors' contributions to the worldwide public domain under CC0 1.0. That is not a blanket worldwide determination for the underlying paintings; check local law before redistribution. Source credit does not imply endorsement.

The covers use League Spartan and Sorts Mill Goudy by The League of Moveable Type, as credited by the source editions. The source SVGs are self-contained and retained unmodified.

The Ambra icon is existing owned project artwork. The Quiet Coast, The Glass Orchard, A Season of Light, their generated covers, the reader passage, notes, and all progress values are original fictional demonstration material. These are not downloaded books or claims about a user's reading history.

The book-information, help, settings, library, contents, and text-options icons use BookInformation24Regular, QuestionCircle24Regular, Settings24Regular, Library24Regular, TextBulletList24Regular, and TextFont24Regular from the existing application's Microsoft Fluent UI React Icons 2.0.341 dependency, under its MIT license. These glyphs are shared across the previews.

The reader controls also use that dependency's Search24Regular, Bookmark24Regular, Bookmark24Filled, Headphones24Regular, Pin24Regular, PinOff24Regular, Play24Regular, and Pause24Regular glyphs under the same license.

Publisher, filename, and file size for the three Standard Ebooks editions were read from the local EPUB metadata/files: Ulysses, 2,223,314 bytes; Through the Looking-Glass, 58,880,234 bytes; The Autobiography of a Super-Tramp, 1,391,441 bytes. Descriptions are original summaries, not copied publisher descriptions. Ambra Sample Editions is a fictional publisher label for the three original samples, whose 3.1 MB sizes are illustrative. The library's used-size total sums these six sample sizes; the 100 GB availability is illustrative. No device storage was measured.

Save as and Inspector are deliberately disconnected. Other publication metadata, including identifiers and accessibility metadata, is not modeled here; its absence from the study is not a proposal to remove those fields from Ambra.
