What counts as a content type?

Proposed definitions: images = img or SVG; headings = h1-h6; tables = table; notes = explicit footnote/endnote semantics; quotations = blockquote or q; links = a[href]. Elements may belong to more than one type.

This study uses a small, hand-authored index, one result per sample element. Content-text search includes sample alt text and captions; source search is literal, case-insensitive text matching. A production whole-book search would also need ordinary prose and other archive text files.

Results are observations, not accessibility findings. The sample is not exhaustive, does not parse an EPUB, and does not change your library or files. All prose and illustrations here are original sample material.
