# Ambra Library

Keep your EPUBs together and reopen a book at your saved reading position. Use the library icon in the reader toolbar to return to your books.

## Add a book

1. In an empty library, choose **Bring a book → Choose EPUB files...** and select EPUBs saved on your device. Once you have books, use **Import EPUB**.
2. Wait for the book to finish importing, then open it to read.
3. Use **Sort** to change the library’s order.

To browse book sources, choose **Find your next book → Explore books** in an empty library, or **Find books** once you have books. Sources open in a new tab:

- **Standard Ebooks:** choose **Advanced epub** for formatted classics.
- **Project Gutenberg:** choose an **EPUB** or **EPUB3** download.
- **ReadBeyond:** choose **Download** for EPUBs with recorded narration, rather than the browser-based **Read+Listen**.
- **[eBooks.com](https://www.ebooks.com/drm-free-epub):** buy a DRM-free EPUB edition and download it after purchase.

Check the book’s license and your local copyright rules.

## Import a browser download

When a supported EPUB download opens Ambra, keep that library tab open while it downloads, prepares, and saves the book. A checkmark and the book’s title confirm completion. Choose **Read now** to open it, or **Dismiss** to clear finished messages without interrupting other imports.

While the status says **Downloading**, **Cancel download** stops Ambra’s download and cancels the paused original download in Chrome. Cancellation is unavailable once preparation or saving begins, and does not apply to files selected from your device. If cancellation cannot be confirmed, check **Chrome Downloads** as directed.

If automatic import fails, follow the error message, then use **Choose EPUB files...** or **Import EPUB** to select the downloaded file. Ambra leaves the original browser download available unless import succeeds or you explicitly cancel it. Invalid EPUBs are identified in the error headline; technical details underneath can help with [reporting a problem](report-issues.md).

Other open Library tabs update when a book is imported or deleted. Each tab keeps its own download status and display choices.

## Check book details

Open **Book details** in the Library or reader to see the book’s description. **Publication details** contains rights and accessibility information. **Show more** expands longer excerpts; an ellipsis means some text is omitted. These are summaries of the supplied text, not interpretations of legal terms. The [EPUB Inspector](epub-inspector.md) shows the original metadata and files without changing the book.

## Keep backups

Your library and reading data stay in your browser profile: there is no cloud library or automatic cross-device library sync. Storage has limits. Removing the extension, clearing its data, or losing the profile can remove your books and reading data. Keep original EPUBs and [annotation exports](annotations-and-bookmarks.md#export-or-import-annotations) as backups.

To save a copy of an imported EPUB:

1. Open **Book details → Publication details** in the Library or reader.
2. Choose **Save as…** beside **File name**.
3. Confirm Chrome’s save dialog, keeping the Ambra tab open until the download starts.

The saved file uses the imported filename and contains the original EPUB already on your device. This does not download the book again or change your library copy, reading position, or annotations. Cancelling the save dialog changes nothing.

Ambra is available in the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ambra-epub-reader/mcjkkebkhifgkkbahlcapjlnaihocogj). See the official [privacy policy](https://ambraepub.org/en/privacy/) for privacy details.
