# Book Navigation

## Contents and search

Choose **Contents** to browse chapters and jump to a passage.

Click or tap the **Search** button or press **Mod+F** to search the book;
enter at least three characters.

**Alt+Page Up / Alt+Page Down** jumps to the previous / next section.

**Mod** means Command on Mac or Control elsewhere; **Alt** is Option on Mac.
See all [keyboard shortcuts](README.md#keyboard-shortcuts).

Page progression follows the book's declared direction. If it is unspecified,
Ambra uses the publication language, so Arabic and Hebrew books progress
right-to-left. This does not change the authored text direction or chapter order.
In a right-to-left spread, the earlier page number is on the right and the
next number is on the left. The progress bar follows the same direction:
the start is on the right, and progress moves left.

## Go to a page or percentage

- **Mod+G:** go to a page.
- **Mod+Shift+G:** go to a percentage.

Available in paginated reflowable books once page counts are ready.
Page numbers change with text size and layout.

## Move along the progress bar

Click or drag the progress bar to move through a paginated book.
While dragging, a preview shows where you'll land.

- Bookmark flags beneath the bar jump to saved places. A flag with a number
  groups nearby bookmarks; choose it to pick a destination.
- Chapter bands and landmarks help you spot sections and the book's main
  text. See [Progress landmarks](progress-landmarks.md).

Hide bands and landmarks through **Ambra settings → Reading preferences →
Progress landmarks** without hiding your bookmarks.

**Tip:** use your browser's **Back** button to return to your previous reading
location after a jump. **Forward** takes you there again.
