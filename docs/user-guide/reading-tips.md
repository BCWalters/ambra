# Reading tips

## Turn pages comfortably

In pagination, click or tap the outer margin on the side you want to move toward. Reading order reverses for right-to-left books. Reflowable text and whitespace inside the reading area do not turn pages. Fixed-layout books also accept taps close to the outside edge of the outermost page, even when artwork fills it. The gap between pages and their inner edges never turn pages.

If reader controls are showing, a page-turning click first hides them; click again to turn. Pinned panels stay open. Links, controls, images, text selection, and deliberate swipes keep their own behavior.

Use **Page Down** or **Space** for the next page, and **Page Up** or **Shift+Space** for the previous page. See the [keyboard shortcuts](README.md#keyboard-shortcuts) for more options.

The first book you open offers a short introduction. Choose **Start reading**, close it, or press **Escape** to continue at the same position. Revisit it through **Settings → Help & About → Reading tips**. Use the library icon in the reader toolbar to return to your books.

## Choose pagination or scrolling

For reflowable books, open **Settings → Reading mode** and choose pagination or scrolling. Scrolling keeps Page Up, Page Down, and Space available for native scrolling; it can be useful for continuous screen-reader reading. Fixed-layout content retains the publisher’s layout.

**Alt+Shift+Page Down** selects scrolling; **Alt+Shift+Page Up** selects pagination. On Mac, use **Option** for Alt; laptops may also need **Fn+Down/Up** for Page Down/Up. Switching modes preserves your reading position, and choosing the current mode leaves it unchanged.

Keep focus in the book for reading navigation. Modified commands such as search can also work from reader toolbar buttons, but text fields, selections, widgets, and dialogs keep their own keys. Ambra does not require a screen reader’s application mode. Screen-reader support is still being tested; [feedback](report-issues.md) is welcome.

## Adjust the reading appearance

- **Book options:** adjust typography and page width for reflowable text. **Book default** preserves publisher typography.
- **Page → Always show one page:** keep reflowable pagination on one centered page instead of a two-page spread. This is saved per book and is off by default. It also applies to reflowable sections in mixed-layout books, but does not change fixed-layout pages or scrolling.
- **Settings → Reader theme:** change the reader interface’s appearance.
- **Settings → Page theme:** choose **White**, **Sepia**, or **Dark** across books. These set the reflowable page background and base text/link colors, including when the publisher supplies page colors. Deliberately colored passages and illustrations retain their styling; fixed-layout artwork is not recolored.
- **Settings → Brightness:** adjust brightness.
- **Settings → Page turn:** choose the page-turn effect.
- **Settings → Language:** choose the interface language.

Settings show the current choice beside each option. In a flyout, use arrow keys to move between choices, Enter or Space to select, and Escape to return to its parent.

## Look closer at images and tables

Open an image that offers zoom to inspect it in the image viewer. Use **Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, or **Fit to window**. Scroll to zoom; drag or use arrow keys to pan. Within the viewer, **+**, **−**, and **0** zoom in, zoom out, and fit to the window. Close it or press **Escape** to return to reading.

In reflowable books, choose **Expand table** at a table’s upper edge to open a separate viewer. Zoom the whole table and scroll in both directions without changing the reading page. Fixed-layout pages do not offer table expansion. See the detailed [Table viewer guide](table-viewer.md) for controls and limitations.

## Read popup notes without losing your place

Supported footnote and endnote references open a read-only popup rather than taking you away from the passage. Close it or press **Escape** to continue reading. This depends on how the EPUB marks its note references; other links may navigate normally.

Moving over footnote, selection, or highlight popup controls does not bring the reader toolbar or progress bar over them. Typing a personal note stays in the editor. To keep your own notes, see [Annotations and Bookmarks](annotations-and-bookmarks.md).

## Listen to supplied narration

When an EPUB includes recorded narration, use its playback controls to listen with synchronized text highlighting. This uses the book’s supplied narration, not automatic text-to-speech; books without narration do not gain audio playback.
