# Reading tips

## Turn the page

Click or tap on the outer margins to turn left or right in the book.

Use **Page Down** or **Space** for the next page, and **Page Up** or **Shift+Space** for the previous page. See the [keyboard shortcuts](README.md#keyboard-shortcuts) for more options.

## Choose pagination or scrolling

Open **Ambra settings → Reading preferences → Reading mode** to choose pagination
or scrolling. Scrolling can be useful for continuous screen-reader reading.
Fixed-layout books keep their original layout.

Use **Alt+Shift+Page Down** for scrolling or **Alt+Shift+Page Up** for pagination (**Option** instead of Alt on Mac).

## Adjust the reading appearance

For reflowable books, **Book options** (Aa) changes text size, typography,
and page width. Choose **Book default** to use the publisher's typography.
Prefer a single page? Turn on **Page → Always show one page**.
These choices are saved separately for each book.

**Ambra settings** (gear) applies across books: choose your language,
interface theme, page color, brightness, and page-turn effect. Page color
is under **Reading preferences → Page theme**; it is separate from the
interface theme. Light or dark interface appearance follows your browser.

## Look closer at images and tables

Click an image that offers zoom for a closer look. Scroll to zoom and drag
to move around; **0** fits it to the window and **Escape** returns to reading.

For a wide table, choose **Expand table** at its upper edge to zoom and scroll
without changing the page. See the [Table viewer guide](table-viewer.md).

## The best of EPUB

Footnotes and endnotes can open beside the passage without leaving the page.
For your own notes, see [Annotations and Bookmarks](annotations-and-bookmarks.md).

If your book includes recorded narration, [read along](read-along-books.md)
with synchronized text highlighting.
