# Third-party materials and fixture provenance

The [MIT license](LICENSE) covers Ambra's original code, documentation, artwork,
and original synthetic test fixtures. Store screenshots have additional
source-specific terms described below. MIT does not relicense dependencies, books,
book covers, downloaded descriptions, fonts, or other third-party works.

## Dependencies

Production dependencies are permissively licensed. The installed lockfile
inventory on 2026-09-23 (`pnpm licenses list --prod`) reports:

| License | Packages |

Each dependency remains under its own license. Distributable bundles must include
the applicable copyright, license, and NOTICE texts from the exact installed
versions, including transitive dependencies. This summary is not a replacement
for those texts. Recheck the inventory whenever the lockfile changes.
Version-specific, upstream-verified fallbacks for packages omitting license files
are documented in [store-assets/licenses](store-assets/licenses/README.md).

## Checked-in test data and original artwork

- `packages/engine/test/fixtures/*-src/` contains small synthetic EPUB documents,
  packaged by `packages/engine/scripts/build-fixtures.sh`. Deliberately malformed
  books, script probes, and the tiny PDF are test inputs, not real publications.
- The engine's 2×2 and 4×4 PNGs and 300×800 test image are synthetic fixtures.
  `content-loader-epub-src/OEBPS/fonts/font.otf` is dummy data, not a font.
  The obfuscated `.otf` files and plaintext `.bin` contain deterministic test
  bytes generated by `generate-font-obfuscation-fixture.mjs`, not licensed fonts.
- `apps/e2e/fixtures/` contains synthetic test publications. Long-content and
  embedded-annotation fixtures reuse the engine's synthetic content. Media-overlay
  WAV files are generated quiet tones/silence, not third-party narration; see
  `apps/e2e/scripts/generate-media-overlay-fixtures.mjs`. Other generators in that
  directory document reading-boundary, disclosure, and scale test content.
- `apps/extension/icon-source/icon.svg`, the generated extension PNG icons, and
  the Ambra promo tile are project artwork. The promo tile's source is
  `store-assets/scripts/generate-images.mjs`.

## External books and metadata

Real books belong outside source control, normally in ignored
`apps/e2e/real-books/`. Download availability does not grant redistribution rights.
Public-domain status of a text does not settle rights in an edition's cover,
illustrations, narration, fonts, or packaging, and varies by jurisdiction.

- Project Gutenberg downloads retain their own notices and terms.
- The W3C/IDPF Moby-Dick media-overlay sample is CC BY-SA; check the exact
  downloaded package's license and attribution requirements.
- ReadBeyond's *A Horseman in the Sky* EPUB package is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, not MIT.
  Do not include it in Ambra's source or extension distribution.
- Open Library and Wikipedia descriptions retain their source terms. Ambra
  records a source link; that does not transfer ownership or grant an MIT
  sublicense. Review attribution/license requirements before redistributing
  fetched text, including in screenshots.

## Existing store screenshots

The screenshots contain official IDPF/W3C samples and Project Gutenberg material,
not unlicensed personal books. [Screenshot attributions](store-assets/ATTRIBUTIONS.md)
record the exact sources, copyright notices, CC BY-SA 3.0 collection license,
Israel Sailing's explicit CC BY-SA 2.5 terms, and Gutenberg public-domain/reuse
basis. The screenshots are not wholly MIT-licensed: retain their source credits
and license links when redistributing them.

The screenshots also exist in Git history (introduced in `a006795`). These
attributions apply to retained copies of the same images. Primary-source research
supports attribution and license compliance rather than presuming history
rewriting is necessary. Public-domain status is jurisdiction-dependent; the
source notice is not a worldwide legal guarantee. New store screenshots using
original synthetic books remain the simplest option. The existing generator
imports external books and is not an attribution-free asset pipeline.
