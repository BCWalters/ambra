### Historical verification and limits

Reviewed on 2026-09-23 using primary sources:

- The [official release-time README](https://github.com/IDPF/epub3-samples/blob/20230704/README.md)
  and [official sample table](https://idpf.github.io/epub3-samples/30/samples.html)
  license samples CC BY-SA 3.0 unless otherwise specified. The Accessible EPUB 3,
  Children's Literature, and Internal Links entries list no contrary exception.
  A copyright notice alone is not a prohibition on licensed redistribution.
- The local Accessible EPUB 3, Children's Literature, and Internal Links cover
  bytes match the corresponding files in release `20230704`. Israel Sailing's
  embedded copyright text matches the official release source. Alice's EPUB
  cover corresponds to the source ebook's cover, with minor JPEG recompression.
- [CC BY-SA 3.0 section 4](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode.en)
  requires attribution, license notice, and ShareAlike for adaptations. It does
  not require separately collected original software to use CC BY-SA.

This evidence supports retaining and attributing these screenshots rather than
presuming that their presence requires Git history rewriting. It is not a legal
opinion or a worldwide public-domain determination. For store publication,
include these notices with the listing/screenshots or use newly captured original
synthetic books to avoid third-party attribution and jurisdiction complexity.
Do not reuse the image-generation script's external books without preserving
their notices. Do not distribute full EPUBs as part of the extension ZIP.
