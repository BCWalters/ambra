AMBRA / MOTION LAB

A leaf, not a rigid panel.

Comparison prototype for #369 and #370. Original sample pages; not the live EPUB renderer.

Pages 1-2

Click an outer page or use arrow keys. Drag with mouse/touch (a trackpad can click-drag): slow movement follows your hand; release at or past 40% completes; drag back below 40% to return the leaf. The slider freezes any frame for inspection.

The reverse face is the destination page, including artwork. The other page stays still. Real EPUB frame compositing, selection, chapter boundaries and zoom are integration work, not proven by this lab.
